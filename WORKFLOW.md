# Peer Benchmarking Workflow

**How to run an Institutional AI Maturity peer benchmarking study using the [scoring rubric](RUBRIC.md)**

The workflow follows one sequence:

> **Define → Calibrate → Research → Normalize → Interpret → Recommend → Assemble → Audit → Produce → Communicate**

---

## Stage 1 — Define the study

| Step | What happens | Result |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 1 | Set up a single, persistent workspace and load the full seven-dimension framework so every later step uses the same context. | Master framework established |
| 2 | Fix the target institution and the complete peer group before any scoring begins. | Target and peer list fixed |
| 3 | Adopt the 1–5 scoring rubric: score anchors, observable evidence, "Not publicly evident" handling, confidence rules and level boundaries for all seven dimensions. | Operational scoring standard in place |

## Stage 2 — Calibrate the method

| Step | What happens | Result |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 4A | **Calibration batch 1:** apply the fixed rubric to two institutions with contrasting profiles, using current primary-source evidence. | First calibration cases |
| 4B | **Calibration batch 2:** apply the identical rubric to a second, different pair. | Second calibration cases |
| 4C | **Cross-institution review:** compare all four cases for inconsistent scoring, prestige bias, pilot-versus-production confusion and score-boundary ambiguity. | Calibration issues identified |
| 4D | **Adjudication:** resolve flagged issues, complete governance mapping and finalize confidence records. | Rubric and calibration reference frozen |

## Stage 3 — Research and normalize the full peer group

| Step | What happens | Result |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 5 | Research and score the remaining peers in batches, using the frozen rubric, calibration cases, confidence labels and operating-status rules. | Complete peer evidence base |
| 6 | Review every dimension across all institutions to confirm the same evidence thresholds and boundaries were applied. | Normalized dataset and change log |
| 6A | Resolve the last normalization issues; preserve original and final values separately in the evidence ledger. | Dataset ready for analysis |

## Stage 4 — Interpret the benchmark and build the roadmap

| Step | What happens | Result |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 7 | Interpret peer patterns, dimension maturity, leading practices, common gaps, dependencies, emerging baselines, adoption and value realization, and the target institution's position. | Findings package |
| 8 | Convert findings into prioritized recommendations, phased actions, maturity objectives, operating-model implications, investments, success measures and execution risks. | Recommendations and 3-year roadmap |

## Stage 5 — Assemble and quality-control the report

| Step | What happens | Result |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 9 | Combine framework, methodology, peer results, findings, target assessment, recommendations, roadmap, risks, measures and appendices. | Complete report draft |
| 10 | Audit score consistency, confidence, table-to-narrative alignment, recommendation traceability, roadmap sequencing, sources and readability. | Quality-control issue log |
| 10A | Recover or re-verify any missing source-to-score support and confidence labels. | Authoritative evidence ledger |
| 10B | Close the remaining evidence gaps. | Report passes final quality control |

## Stage 6 — Produce final deliverables

| Step | What happens | Result |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 11A | Build the authoritative spreadsheet **first**: final scores, evidence ledger, target analysis, recommendations, roadmap, rubric and audit trail. | Benchmarking workbook |
| 11B | Generate the client-ready PDF **from the spreadsheet**, so every score, confidence label and recommendation stays aligned. | Final PDF report |

## Stage 7 — Create executive communication assets

| Step | What happens | Result |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 12 | One-page executive maturity heatmap: target versus peers, strongest dimensions, largest gaps, top priorities. | Executive heatmap |
| 13 | Simplified heatmap for boards and trustees, showing only the most important comparative signals. | Board/trustee view |
| 14 | One executive takeaway slide: where the institution stands, what constrains progress, top three leadership priorities. | Executive slide |
| 15 | Anonymized thought-leadership content that shares general findings without naming or ranking institutions. | External communication asset |

---

## Key decision gates

| Gate | Proceed only when… |
|:--|:--|
| After Step 3 | The scoring rubric is complete and operational. |
| After Step 4D | Calibration is resolved and the rubric is frozen. |
| After Step 6A | The peer dataset is normalized and the evidence ledger is reconciled. |
| After Step 10B | All material scores are source-supported and quality control passes. |
| Before Step 11B | The authoritative spreadsheet is complete. |
