# Higher Ed AI Maturity Rubric

**An evidence-based standard for benchmarking institutional AI maturity in colleges and universities.**

Most universities can point to AI pilots, tools and task forces. Far fewer can show that AI is governed, supported, adopted and delivering value *as an institution*. This rubric gives leaders a consistent way to tell the difference.

## The seven dimensions

1. **Strategy, Governance & Risk Management**
2. **Data & AI Readiness**
3. **Technology, Platforms & Infrastructure**
4. **People, AI Literacy & Organizational Capacity**
5. **Teaching, Learning & Student Experience**
6. **Research, Innovation & Advanced Computing**
7. **Adoption, Scale & Value Realization**

## The five maturity levels

| Score | Level | Meaning |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 1 | **Initial** | Individual or isolated activity |
| 2 | **Emerging** | Institutional coordination is taking shape |
| 3 | **Developing** | Defined, repeatable capabilities in priority areas |
| 4 | **Advanced** | Coordinated capabilities across material domains, with evaluation |
| 5 | **Leading** | Repeated outcome evidence drives continuous improvement |

## Principles behind the rubric

- **Capability, not visibility.** A website, research center or single tool does not establish maturity.
- **Silence is not a low score.** When public evidence is insufficient, the result is *Not publicly evident*, never a penalty.
- **Confidence is separate from maturity.** Every score carries its own evidence-confidence rating.
- **No prestige bias.** Reputation is never evidence. Calibration happens before any institution is scored.
- **Fixed before scoring.** The rubric is frozen before research begins; any material change requires a new version and full reassessment.

## How to use it

- **Self-assessment:** score your own institution to see where it stands and what blocks the next level in each dimension.
- **Peer benchmarking:** follow [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md) to compare your institution with selected peers.
- **Planning:** use the "What prevents the next level" column in each dimension to shape a multi-year AI roadmap.

This rubric is an analyst-designed benchmarking method. It is not an audit, a certification or an externally validated maturity instrument.
